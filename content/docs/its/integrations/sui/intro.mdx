The [Interchain Token Service](/its/overview) (ITS) runs on [Sui](https://sui.io/) as the Move package [`interchain_token_service`](https://github.com/axelarnetwork/axelar-cgp-sui/tree/v1.2.0/move/interchain_token_service), version 1.2.0 on mainnet and testnet. It sends and receives everything through the [ITS Hub](/its/how-it-works/its-hub), as [GMP messages](/gmp/integrations/sui/intro).

## How a transfer works

Sending, in one transaction:

1. `prepare_interchain_transfer` puts the coins, the destination and an optional payload in an `InterchainTransferTicket`, with your [channel](/gmp/integrations/sui/gmp-contracts#channel) as the sender.
1. `send_interchain_transfer` takes the coins (locks them, or burns them with the coin's `TreasuryCap`) and returns a `MessageTicket` for the ITS Hub.
1. The transaction pays gas for the ticket (`pay_gas`) and sends it (`send_message`), as for any [GMP message](/gmp/integrations/sui/gmp-messages#send-a-message).

Receiving: the ITS Hub forwards the transfer, and Axelar's relayer calls `receive_interchain_transfer` on Sui ITS, which unlocks or mints the coins and sends them to the destination address. A transfer with data goes to a Move package instead; see [Receive coins with data](/its/integrations/sui/its-contracts#receive-coins-with-data).

Every call that goes to another chain (a transfer, a remote deployment, a link, a metadata registration) returns a `MessageTicket` that you pay gas for and send in the same way. Estimate the fee as described in [Estimate ITS fees](/gmp/gas/estimate-and-pay#estimate-its-fees). [Express](/gmp/integrations/evm/express) currently doesn't work for ITS transfers.

## Setup

You need:

- The [Sui CLI](https://docs.sui.io/guides/developer/getting-started/sui-install). The commands on these pages were tested with version 1.83.
- A testnet address with SUI: `sui client faucet`, or see [Get SUI tokens](https://docs.sui.io/guides/developer/getting-started/get-coins).

The guides call ITS with [programmable transactions](https://docs.sui.io/concepts/transactions/prog-txn-blocks) (`sui client ptb`), so they don't need a Move dependency on ITS. To call ITS from your own package, see [Depend on ITS](/its/integrations/sui/its-contracts#depend-on-its).

## Sui specifics

- **Coin management.** On Sui, ITS keeps each coin's token manager inside its own object, as a `CoinManagement`. You create it before registering the coin: with the coin's `TreasuryCap` for mint and burn (`new_with_cap`), or without for lock and unlock (`new_locked`). See [Token managers](/its/how-it-works/concepts#token-managers).
- **Token IDs.** Sui derives token IDs differently from EVM chains: from the coin's type and details for a canonical coin, and from a channel and a salt for a custom coin. They're the same on every chain once registered. See [Token IDs](/its/integrations/sui/its-contracts#token-ids).
- **Channels as identity.** Sui has no `msg.sender`. The sender of a transfer, the deployer of a custom coin and the holders of roles are [channels](/gmp/integrations/sui/gmp-contracts#channel), identified by their object IDs.
- **Decimals and amounts.** Sui amounts are `u64`. When an interchain token is deployed to Sui from a chain with larger amounts, such as an EVM chain, the ITS Hub gives it at most 6 decimals on Sui, and scales amounts between the chains, rounding down. A transfer whose amount would round to zero, or not fit in a `u64`, fails at the hub.
- **Flow limits and roles** work differently from EVM chains: see [Roles](/its/integrations/sui/its-contracts#roles) and [Flow limits](/its/integrations/sui/its-contracts#flow-limits).
