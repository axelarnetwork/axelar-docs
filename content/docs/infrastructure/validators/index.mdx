An Axelar network validator participates in block creation, multi-party cryptography protocols, and voting.

Convert your existing Axelar node into a validator by running the companion processes and staking AXL tokens. Once it is running, your validator can also [verify legacy chains](/infrastructure/verifiers/legacy-chains).

## Prerequisites

- **Hardware:** Minimum: 16 cores, 16GB RAM, 1.5 TB drive. Recommended: 32 cores, 32 GB RAM, 2 TB+ drive.
- A fully synced Axelar node (see [Sync a node](/infrastructure/node/sync)), and familiarity with [Basic node management](/infrastructure/node/basic).

## Steps to become a validator

1. [Set up vald and tofnd](/infrastructure/validators/setup/vald-tofnd): install the companion processes, create your accounts and keys, and launch them.
2. [Register and stake](/infrastructure/validators/setup/register-and-stake): register your broadcaster account and stake AXL.
3. [Run a health check](/infrastructure/validators/operations/health-check).
4. Optionally, [verify legacy chains](/infrastructure/verifiers/legacy-chains) to earn additional rewards.

Prefer to set everything up by hand, or recovering a validator from its mnemonic and keys? See [Manual setup](/infrastructure/validators/setup/manual).

If something goes wrong, see [Troubleshooting](/infrastructure/validators/troubleshooting). To stop being a validator, see [Leave the network](/infrastructure/validators/operations/leave).
