## Add gas

If the gas you paid isn't enough, for example because the destination gas price rose, the message waits on Axelarscan with an **Insufficient fee** status. Add gas to it on the source chain, and it continues.

The easiest way is the **Add gas** button on the message's Axelarscan page, or the AxelarJS SDK. See [Recover a message](/gmp/track-and-recover/recover-transactions#add-gas).

To do it from a contract or script, call the gas service on the source chain. The message is identified by the hash of the transaction that sent it and the index of its `ContractCall` event log in that transaction:

```solidity
function addNativeGas(
    bytes32 txHash,
    uint256 logIndex,
    address refundAddress
) external payable;
```

| Message | Native token | ERC-20 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | `addNativeGas` | `addGas` |
| [Express](/gmp/integrations/evm/express) | `addNativeExpressGas` | `addExpressGas` |

The ERC-20 variants take a `gasToken` and `gasFeeAmount`, and need an approval first. Gas you add that isn't used is refunded like the rest.

## Refunds

Gas left after execution is refunded on the source chain to the refund address you set when paying gas ([how refunds are calculated](/gmp/gas/gas-service#refunds)). Refunds are sent in the token you paid with, except on [XRPL](/gmp/integrations/xrpl#refunds), where they're in XRP.

The refund address is the last argument of every payment function:

```solidity
gasService.payNativeGasForContractCall{ value: msg.value }(
    address(this),
    destinationChain,
    destinationAddress,
    payload,
    msg.sender // refund address
);
```

`msg.sender` refunds the user who called your contract. To refund somewhere else, pass that address instead, or let the caller choose by taking it as a parameter. If you pass `address(this)`, your contract receives the refund, so it needs a way to receive native tokens (a `receive` function) and to withdraw them.

Each refund emits a `Refunded` event on the gas service, and appears on the message's Axelarscan page.
