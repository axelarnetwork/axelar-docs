General Message Passing (GMP) lets a contract on one chain call a contract on any other chain connected to Axelar. With GMP you can:

- call a contract on chain B from chain A,
- call a contract on chain B from chain A and attach gateway tokens (consensus EVM chains only; to move any other token, use the [Interchain Token Service](/its/overview)).

<Callout emoji="🚨">
  The security of your app is limited by the security of the chains it
  integrates with. Do your due diligence on every chain your contracts are
  deployed to.
</Callout>

## How a message is delivered

1. **Source chain:** your contract calls the Axelar gateway (on EVM chains, `callContract`) and pays gas for the whole route to the gas service.
2. **Axelar:** the message is verified against the source chain, by Axelar validators for consensus chains or by Amplifier verifiers for Amplifier chains, and routed to the destination chain.
3. **Destination chain:** the gateway records the approved message, and a relayer calls your destination contract, which checks the approval with the gateway before running its logic.

You can follow any message on Axelarscan ([mainnet](https://axelarscan.io/gmp) | [testnet](https://testnet.axelarscan.io/gmp)). If it gets stuck, see [Track and recover](/gmp/track-and-recover/transaction-states). For the details of each step, see [Message flow](/gmp/how-it-works/message-flow) and [Message verification](/gmp/how-it-works/message-verification).

- [Axelarscan](https://axelarscan.io/gmp): track messages, and recover them if they get stuck.
- [AxelarJS SDK](/gmp/resources/axelarjs-sdk): query and recover messages, and estimate gas, from JavaScript.
- [Examples and tutorials](/gmp/resources/examples-and-tutorials): sample apps, a local development environment and step-by-step tutorials.
